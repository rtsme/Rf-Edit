Just open the TimerItem.xlsx file.

Go to the ItemTimer tab, which is the client side.

Find the items and set Type to 0 and Time to 0.

But only do this on the client side, because the server side will still keep its time and expiration.

Don't touch the server-side TimerItem.txt, since it controls whether it expires and its duration.

Generate itemtimer.dat, convert it to edf, and replace it in the client's DataTable.
